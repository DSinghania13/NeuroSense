IU1 — Time

Definition: Specific day or time reference
Examples: “Monday morning”, “Tuesday evening”, “last Friday”

⸻

IU2 — Person

Definition: Main character (named individual)
Examples: Ravi, Anita, Mohit

⸻

IU3 — Location

Definition: Place where the event begins
Examples: market, shop, bakery, grocery store

⸻

IU4 — Proximity

Definition: Spatial relation of the location
Examples: near his house, close to her apartment

⸻

IU5 — Purpose

Definition: Reason for going to the location
Examples: to buy groceries, to purchase food, to get supplies

⸻

IU6 — Action-1

Definition: First concrete action performed
Examples: bought vegetables, purchased fruits

⸻

IU7 — Action-2

Definition: Second concrete action performed
Examples: bought bread, purchased rice

⸻

IU8 — Action-3

Definition: Third concrete action performed
Examples: bought milk, purchased oil

⸻

IU9 — Interaction

Definition: Social interaction with another person
Examples: met a neighbor, met a friend, met a colleague

⸻

IU10 — Interaction Topic

Definition: Subject of conversation during interaction
Examples: weather, work, family, daily life

⸻

IU11 — Duration

Definition: Time span of interaction
Examples: a few minutes, a short time, briefly

⸻

IU12 — Return

Definition: Returning to home
Examples: returned home, came back home, reached home

⸻

IU13 — Home Activity

Definition: Activity performed after returning home
Examples: prepared breakfast, cooked lunch, cooked dinner

⸻

IU14 — Meal / Routine

Definition: Eating or routine daily action
Examples: ate breakfast, had dinner, rested

⸻

IU15 — Ending Context

Definition: Final event or closure of the story
Examples: left for work, went to rest, relaxed
